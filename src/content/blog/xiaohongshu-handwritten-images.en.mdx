# Handwritten quote cards for social media, done in a browser

Scroll any social platform and you'll notice: a handwritten quote card often out-performs a carefully edited photo. Handwriting carries a sense of honesty — perfect for reflective, literary and book-quote accounts.

The problem is producing them consistently. Writing by hand is slow, scanning is a chore, and typing-based generators lose the human touch. Here's the voice-input workflow:

## One card, start to finish

1. Open the [tool](/) and **speak the quote** — spoken phrasing actually sounds more like a handwriting creator;
2. Font **Ma Shan Zheng**, paper **Blank** (no lines for social images — more whitespace, easier to crop);
3. Ink **Black**, realism 60%, export PNG;
4. In your social app, crop to 3:4 and add a slight warm filter. That's the "handwriting creator" look.

## A batch rhythm that works

- Record 5–10 quotes in one sitting, one paragraph each, then crop them one by one;
- Lock the same font + realism + ink combo — that consistency becomes your account's "handwriting identity";
- Be careful with "Shuffle strokes" — an identity wants the *same* person's writing, not a new style every card.

## Cover and layout cheat sheet

- **Cover**: quote + handwriting + lots of whitespace beats a crammed layout;
- **First slide**: handwritten card plus one plain-font line of context;
- **Keywords**: include "handwritten quotes", "handwriting" and similar terms for search traffic.

## FAQ

**Can I use this for a business account?** Yes — exported images are yours. Follow platform rules and don't impersonate another person's handwriting.

**Mine looks too neat?** Raise realism to ~70% and shuffle a few times.

**Vertical text?** The tool outputs horizontal lines; export and rotate in your design tool.

## Tested, with honest limitations

Every step was tested on **desktop Chrome (macOS, October 2026)**; screenshots are from real runs. What you should know:

- Speech recognition is provided by the browser vendor (Chrome uses Google's service); quality varies with environment, accent and microphone. Always proofread important text;
- Audio-file transcription downloads an ~80MB model on first use, then works offline;
- We collect no text or audio — see the [Privacy Policy](/privacy).

Technical reference: [MDN · Web Speech API](https://developer.mozilla.org/docs/Web/API/Web_Speech_API).
