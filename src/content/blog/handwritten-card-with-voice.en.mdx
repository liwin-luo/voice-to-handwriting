# Write a greeting card with your voice in 3 minutes

A handwritten card lands differently from a printed one. Most of us rarely write by hand anymore — and when we do, it's not pretty.

This tutorial solves it in a browser: **speak your wishes out loud**, the tool transcribes them in real time and renders them as realistic handwriting, then you export an image or a printable PDF.

## Steps (3 minutes total)

1. Open the [voice to handwriting tool](/), tap "Tap to speak", allow microphone access;
2. Say your card message at a normal pace — the text appears as you talk, and you can fix any word right in the editor;
3. In the style panel pick **Ma Shan Zheng** (the steadiest handwriting font), paper **Letter**, ink blue-black or vermilion;
4. Hit "Export PNG" for an image, or "Export PDF" to print.

## Card copy ideas (just say these)

- **For parents:** "Wishing you health and peace. We'll come visit often."
- **For a friend:** "Another year — may every effort pay off, and everything you want still be within reach."
- **For a partner:** "Seeing these words is like seeing me. Thank you for this year, and every year ahead."
- **For colleagues:** "Grateful to be on this journey together. Here's to what's next."

## Three details that make it look real

- **Set realism to 50–70%**: higher looks sloppy, lower looks printed;
- Tap **"Shuffle strokes"** a few times and keep the most natural variation;
- When printing the PDF, choose **actual size** so the printer doesn't shrink the line spacing.

## FAQ

**The print looks small?** Raise the font size above 32px before exporting.

**Mixed English?** Just speak it — English renders in the handwritten Caveat style.

**Sending to a phone?** The exported PNG works as-is in any messaging app.

## Tested, with honest limitations

Every step was tested on **desktop Chrome (macOS, October 2026)**; screenshots are from real runs. What you should know:

- Speech recognition is provided by the browser vendor (Chrome uses Google's service); quality varies with environment, accent and microphone. Always proofread important text;
- Audio-file transcription downloads an ~80MB model on first use, then works offline;
- We collect no text or audio — see the [Privacy Policy](/privacy).

Technical reference: [MDN · Web Speech API](https://developer.mozilla.org/docs/Web/API/Web_Speech_API).
