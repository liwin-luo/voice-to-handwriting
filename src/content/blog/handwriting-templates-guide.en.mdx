# Handwriting template library: six scenarios, pick one and write

"Open the tool → face a blank page → have no idea what to write." The template library exists for exactly that moment. Each template = a scenario-fit text + a matching handwriting style (font, paper, ink, alignment). Pick one, change a few words, export.

## The six scenarios

| Template | Best for | Style pairing |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Love letter | Words you can't say aloud | Running script, letter paper, blue-black |
| Apology | A sincere sorry | Regular script, letter paper, black |
| Thank-you | People who helped you | Running script, letter paper, blue-black |
| Birthday | The birthday post | Regular script, blank paper, vermilion, centered |
| Teachers' Day | Thanking a mentor | Regular script, letter paper, black |
| New Year | The year-end post | Big regular script, blank paper, vermilion, centered |

## Why templates ship with styles

In our testing, right text + wrong style still produces an "off" image: an apology in cursive looks flippant, a birthday wish in black ink loses the festivity. So every template pre-sets font, paper, ink, alignment and realism — all you change is the salutation and signature.

## How to use

1. Pick a scenario in the [template library](/templates);
2. The preview shows the handwritten effect — click "Use this template";
3. The text lands in the editor, fully editable; styles remain adjustable;
4. Export PNG to send, or PDF to print.

## One suggestion

Templates are a starting point, not the destination: replace "friend" and "teacher" with real names, add one small detail only you two know — that's where handwriting earns its value.

## Tested, with honest limitations

Every step was tested on **desktop Chrome (macOS, October 2026)**; screenshots are from real runs. Speech recognition is provided by the browser vendor and quality varies — proofread important text. We collect no text or audio — see the [Privacy Policy](/privacy). Technical reference: [MDN · Web Speech API](https://developer.mozilla.org/docs/Web/API/Web_Speech_API).
