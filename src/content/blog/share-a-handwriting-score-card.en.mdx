# How to share a handwriting score card

Free, no signup. Upload one photo on the handwriting check, pick a frame, and download the PNG. I did this in desktop Chrome on macOS, October 13, 2026. The card was on screen in about half a minute.

## Two lines on a screen

I did not photograph a notebook. In the browser I drew two ruled lines and set the words in Bradley Hand:

Thank you, Mrs. Clark

The class loved the story.

I uploaded that JPEG on the [handwriting check](/handwriting-check). The page said 3 checks were left for the day. After this one, it said 2.

Legibility came back 4.6. Size 4, baseline 4, spacing 5, slant 4. Three of the causes were tied at 4, and the card still marked baseline. The line under the numbers said to practice baseline next. On a tie the page names baseline first, then size, then spacing, then slant.

The notes on the right quoted both sentences and said the feet of "The class loved the story." sit a little below the printed line. Beside 4.6 the page also prints "Ahead of 92% of people." That line is the label this score gets on the page. I did not count anyone.

![Seal score card beside the notes. Two ruled lines, legibility 4.6, baseline marked](/blog/share-a-handwriting-score-card.png)

## Four frames, same numbers

Under the card are Seal, Certificate, Paper, and Plain. I clicked each one in that same session. The scores stayed put. Download saves the frame that is selected, as `handwriting-check.png`. The notes on the right are not in the file.

Seal is the navy card. A red stamp sits on the photo: CERTIFIED along the top, 4.6 in the middle, HANDWRITING along the bottom. I saved this frame the same day and opened the PNG. The navy edge and the red stamp were both in the file.

## Copy the notes, not the card

The card is a picture. The column beside it is text. I selected the paragraph and the five rows, including the line about the second sentence dropping under the rule.

The share row (X, Facebook, WhatsApp, Pinterest, email) opens that site with the legibility line and a link back to this page. It does not upload the PNG. If you want the picture on the post, download it and attach it yourself.

## When the card does not appear

A blurry, cropped, or glared photo asks for another shot. Writing that is not Latin does not get this card. Fewer than two full lines can come back with only legibility, and the other four cells stay blank. Three checks a day in this browser, then the count resets the next day.

This is an impression from one still photo. It is not a clinical grade, and there is no total. A screen font, which is what I uploaded, is not the same thing as a pencil on paper.

The link under the rows, [how to improve your handwriting](/blog/how-to-improve-handwriting-adults), is a four-week practice plan. It does not score the photo again.
