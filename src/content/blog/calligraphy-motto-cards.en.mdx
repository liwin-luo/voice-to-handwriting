## The workflow, with the settings I tested

Ten minutes includes picking the motto. Here's the exact sequence from an empty page to a finished card:

1. **Open the [handwriting tool](/)** and type your motto into the editor. Two lines works best — the motto, then an em-dash attribution or a small second thought. You can also tap **Tap to speak** and dictate it; fix any recognition slips in the editor, that's what it's for;
2. **Font: Dancing Script · Cursive.** Of the three English calligraphy hands in the font list, this is the formal-flowing one. Cedarville Cursive is the sparse, pen-and-ink antique hand; Caveat is the casual quick script. All three render as real handwriting styles;
3. **Size 36px, Realism 60%.** Realism controls how much the strokes vary from letter to letter — 60% reads as natural repetition; push it to 70–80% when you want a looser, more lived-in hand;
4. **Paper: Blank. Ink: Blue black** (the first swatch). Blue-black is the fountain-pen blue that photographs as "calligraphy"; Vermilion for a seal-stamp accent, Brown for a vintage feel;
5. **Preview → export.** The preview gives you **Copy image** (paste straight into a chat or draft), **Download this page** (full-resolution PNG), and **Download Pinterest pin (2:3)** — a pre-cropped vertical card for boards. The export watermark toggle in the preview turns the small site credit off; for a clean card, switch it off first.

## Tuning table

| You want | Settings |
|---|---|
| Formal, framing-worthy | Dancing Script · 32–36px · Realism 50–60% · Blue black |
| Loose and spontaneous | Caveat · 40px+ · Realism 70–80% · Black |
| Vintage fountain pen | Cedarville Cursive · 34px · Realism 65% · Brown |
| A consistent set for a feed | Same font + size for all cards, press **Shuffle strokes** before each export so no two are identical |

The tuning that surprises people most is **Shuffle strokes**: because the render jitters each stroke deliberately, two exports of the same motto are never pixel-identical. That's a feature for feeds — ten quote cards from one setting, each subtly its own.

## Tested, with honest limitations

Tested on desktop Chrome, macOS, October 9, 2026, with the exact settings in step 2–4:

- This is **font-based handwriting rendering** — a calligraphy *style*, not a scan of anyone's pen. It reads as handwritten at card size; don't expect pressure variation within a stroke;
- Exports genuinely vary (Realism + Shuffle), so "download twice, pick the better one" is a real technique, not superstition;
- The Pinterest pin is a fixed **2:3 crop** of the same page — if your motto runs long, the crop may clip it; keep cards to two lines or export the full page instead;
- Voice input needs desktop Chrome/Edge and microphone permission; typing works in every browser. Nothing you type is uploaded — rendering happens locally.
