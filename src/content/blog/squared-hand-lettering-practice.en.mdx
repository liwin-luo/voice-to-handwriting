# Practice squared handwriting for fifteen minutes a day

I wanted capitals that sit in a box, the way notes on a drawing look. The [architect lettering practice sheets](/architect-lettering) use Architects Daughter, the face Kimberly Geswein published under the [SIL Open Font License](https://scripts.sil.org/OFL). [Google Fonts](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Architects+Daughter) describes it as inspired by the writing of the daughter of an architect: squared, and still a handwriting. It is that open font. Paid fonts sold under a similar name are different files.

## Look at your capitals first

| What you see | What it means | Fifteen minutes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Letters lean a different way in each word | The slant is unset | Trace one solid row of A through J and keep the same lean |
| Gaps between letters jump | Spacing | Trace the dotted row and leave the gap you see on the model |
| The bottoms bounce | The baseline | Stay on the printed line. The blank row comes after two clean dotted rows |
| You want a machine stencil | A different hand | This sheet keeps the handwritten square |

Single-stroke drafting templates and school handwriting programs draw their own letters. This page prints Architects Daughter: three rows of capitals and the digits 0–9. Each line is solid, then dotted, then blank.

## One week

| Day | On the sheet |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | A through J. Finger on the solid row five times, then once with a pencil |
| 2 | The dotted row of the same letters |
| 3 | K through T, the same two steps |
| 4 | U through Z, then the digits |
| 5 | Blank row, model covered, one line you actually write: your name, a street, a room number |
| 6 | That same line, slower, on [lined paper](/printable-paper) |
| 7 | Three copies side by side. Same height, same slant. Keep the two that match |

Four to six weeks of this makes the capitals consistent enough to read. Matching a drafter's template is a longer road, and this font stays a handwriting. Fifteen minutes is the dose. A Sunday pass through the whole alphabet teaches rush, and the square collapses.

Your own capitals will still look like you. The font is the model for the month.
