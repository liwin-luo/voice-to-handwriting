# How two cursive letters connect, same way or a turn

A child who can write a cursive t and a cursive h still stops between them. The join is the stroke in the middle. Same-direction pairs keep traveling the same way. Turning pairs change direction in that middle stroke.

## Same way: th

Leave [cursive letter joins](/cursive-letter-joins) on Same direction and print only th, in Sacramento. The first row is the model. The rows under it are dotted.

Put a finger on the stroke between t and h. It leaves t and enters h without the hand turning back. Then put the pencil on the dots, one page. Watch the pencil. If it lifts between the two letters, that page did not practice the join. The other pairs can wait.

Ages 6, once cursive has started, stay on this kind of pair: th, then ll, then ee. The hand keeps going the same direction.

## A turn: ai

ai is in the other set. The stroke between a and i changes direction in the middle. That is the turn. Ages 7–8 can add it after the same-direction pairs feel ordinary: oo, ss, ch, sh, wh, then ai.

Print ai on its own page. Switch the same sheet to Change direction. Finger the middle of the pair before the pencil moves. If the line between the letters reverses and the child rushes, the pair turns into a scribble. Cover the model after two clean dotted rows. A full word on day one teaches the hand to rush the join.

| Age | Pair | What the hand does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 6, once cursive has started | th, then ll, ee | Same direction. One pair, one page. |
| 7–8 | oo, ss, ch, sh, wh, then ai | Same-direction pairs first. ai is the first turn. |
| 9+ | A word they already use, on [cursive worksheets](/cursive-worksheets) | The pair pages are the warm-up. The word is the test. |

Same direction on the sheet: th, ch, sh, wh, oo, ee, ll, ss. Change direction: ai, ay, ou, ow, oa, ea, ir, er. Single letters stay on the [cursive alphabet](/cursive-alphabet).

## Tomorrow morning

Same direction. Print th. Finger, then pencil, one page. Stop when the pencil stays down between the letters. Do not open the turning set.

## When the join turns into a scribble

Go back to a pair they can already do, usually ll or ee. The pages are free, with no account.
