# Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-08. This policy explains how data is handled when you use Voice to Handwriting.

## 1. Data we do not collect

- **Text content:** text you type or dictate exists only in your browser's memory and local storage (localStorage) — including anything you save to history, which stays on your device. It is never sent to our servers.
- **Exported files:** PNG / PDF files are generated locally by your browser and never pass through us.
- **Accounts:** the site has no account system and requires no registration.

## 2. Third-party processing of voice

Speech recognition uses the browser's native Web Speech API, provided by the browser vendor:

- Desktop Chrome / Edge: audio is sent to Google's speech recognition service, governed by the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy);
- Safari on Apple devices: audio is processed by Apple's speech recognition service.

That data never passes through this site's servers. For fully local use, type your text instead of speaking.

## 3. Local storage

All of the following stays in your browser and never leaves your device; clearing your browser data removes it:

- **Style preferences:** font, paper and other options (`vth-prefs`);
- **History:** if you use the history feature, your saved texts are stored locally (`vth-history`) and can be deleted individually or all at once on the History page;
- **Cookie choice:** your decision in the cookie banner (`vth-consent`).

## 4. Logs and analytics

The site is served by a third-party hosting provider (Vercel), which processes standard request logs (such as IP address and browser type) to deliver pages and prevent abuse. We also use Vercel Web Analytics, a cookieless analytics tool that measures aggregate traffic without cookies and without identifying individual visitors. This data is not used for advertising.

## 5. Cookies and third-party advertising

This site serves ads through third-party networks such as Google AdSense to stay free. These vendors may use cookies to personalize ads. You can manage personalized ads via [Google Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com/) or disable third-party cookies in your browser.

## 6. Children

The site is for general audiences and does not knowingly collect information from children under 14.

## 7. Changes to this policy

Material changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
