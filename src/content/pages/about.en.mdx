# About Voice to Handwriting

Voice to Handwriting is a free web tool: speak into your microphone, watch the transcript appear in real time, and see it rendered as realistic handwriting on paper — ready to export as PNG or PDF.

## Why we built it

You want a handwritten card, a journal entry, or a handwritten quote image — but typing it out and laying it up is a chore. Every other handwriting tool starts with typing. Speaking is always faster. We put voice input and handwriting rendering together, which no comparable tool does.

## Privacy is the default

- Your text and exports are processed entirely in your browser — never uploaded to our servers;
- Speech recognition is provided by your browser's native Web Speech API: desktop Chrome / Edge sends audio to Google's service, Safari on Apple devices uses Apple's — that step never touches us;
- Font and paper preferences live only in your browser's localStorage.

See the [Privacy Policy](/privacy) for details.

## The desks behind the guides

Our guides are signed by five editorial desks — pen names, each with its own beat and voice: classroom practice, adult practice, science, cards & letters, and the tools themselves. Desks are editorial voices, not individual staff profiles.
