## FAQ

### Speech recognition doesn't respond?

Use desktop Chrome / Edge, allow microphone access (icon left of the address bar), and check your system microphone. On other browsers, type directly in the text box.

### The transcript has mistakes?

Edit the text box on the right — the handwriting updates in real time.

### The exported PDF won't open?

Update your browser and retry; for multi-page exports, wait until all pages are generated.

### Business inquiries

For advertising, partnerships or API access, mention "business" in your email.
